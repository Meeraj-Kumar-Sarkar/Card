# Card Classes and Character Classification

This document classifies all cards into five combat classes based on their **battle role, lore implication, and mechanical identity**, not merely rarity.

## Class Definitions

**Hawk**  
Sheer strength and agility. Precision attackers focused on speed, execution, and direct damage.

**Wolf**  
Intelligence and analysis. Support and control units that manipulate flow, buffs, debuffs, and tactics.

**Bear**  
Destructive brute force. Tanks and breakers built for endurance, frontline dominance, and raw impact.

**Sparrow**  
Deep knowledge and magic. Mages specializing in mana control, healing, curses, and reality influence.

**Lion**  
Special class. Rule-breakers, commanders, and narrative-level entities that do not follow standard class logic.

## Design Note

Lion-class characters are not stat upgrades of other classes.  
They operate on **rule overrides, global effects, command mechanics, or reality manipulation**. Treating them as normal units will break balance and waste their narrative weight.
